"blume": minor
---

Blog posts (`type: blog`) now get the support the changelog has. Blume generates a `/blog` index that lists every post newest first, grouped by year, as cards with each post's date, reading time, title, summary, authors, tags, and cover, and a page per tag at `/blog/tags/<tag>`, linked from a tag filter above the list. A post shows its date, reading time, and tags above its title, and its authors (with their avatars) and cover below it, and links the newer and older posts at its foot, with the posts that share its tags suggested when it names no `related` pages. In the sidebar, posts sort newest first and fold into collapsible years, and their titles wrap to a second line instead of being cut off. New front matter: `tags` and `cover` (a site that already declared either through `frontmatter.extend` or a content type's `frontmatter` keeps its own). New config: `blog` (the index's `title` and `description`, and `sidebar.groupBy` and `sidebar.width`), the same `sidebar` options for `changelog` (ungrouped by default), and `seo.rss.content` to put each item's whole page in a feed. A post without a `description` is summarized by its first paragraph. The blog's feed adds each post's authors and tags, `/blog.md` and MCP `get_page` serve the index, `<Blog />` places the list on a hand-written page, its OG card shows the authors, the date, and the cover, and its JSON-LD carries its tags as `keywords`. Custom pages read the posts and tags from `blume:data`'s `blog` (`BlumeBlogPost`, `BlogTagCount`).

For sites that already have blog posts or a changelog: a site with blog posts now gets the `/blog` index and tag pages, unless pages (dynamic ones under `pages/blog/` too, though not a site-wide `pages/[...slug].astro`) or redirects of its own hold those routes, and a `/blog` tab opens that index, in every language and version, as a `/changelog` tab opens the timeline. `blume validate` now checks a page's `cover` and its authors' `avatar` (or `image`) paths that name an image file as it does body images, and a page's `.md` and `.mdx` mirrors point one beside the page at its served URL. Blog posts sort newest first and group by year in the sidebar and MCP `get_navigation` (`blog.sidebar.groupBy: false` lists them flat), by date even where a numeric filename prefix (`01-intro.md`) ordered them before, and ahead of any page beside them ordered by hand (`sidebar.order`, a `meta.ts` `pages` list), as in a changelog folder, where a closed year loads its posts when it opens, as a collapsed group does; changelog entries' titles wrap there too. A dated post's previous and next links lead to the newer and older dated posts; an undated one keeps the sidebar's. A page that is a blog post through `content.defaultType` now gets a post's byline, footer, and `BlogPosting` structured data too. Blog posts file under the blog's title in search, a hosted search adapter's record takes a page's `tags` after its `search.tags`, llms.txt lists them without year headings and summarizes one without a description by its first paragraph, and the site skill links `/blog.md` in place of each post the generated index lists. Every feed credits a page's `authors` and lists its `tags`, and the blog's feed, titled after the blog, lists the default locale's current posts only. With `seo.datePublished: "git"`, an undated changelog entry or blog post is dated by its git date in the sidebar, the index, and the feed too, as its page already was. A changelog date's `<time datetime>`, and its date and year heading in `/changelog.md`, name the day in `dateFormat.timeZone`, as its label does. The new blog strings are translated in every built-in UI pack.
