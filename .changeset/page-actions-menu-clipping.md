A page actions menu (Export, Open in chat, Connect to MCP) now wraps a label too long for the right rail, as in many of the built-in translations (French, Portuguese, Russian, Swedish, and others) or with a long custom MCP client label. Before, the menu could reach past the rail's edge, and the rail, which scrolls, cut it off. A wrapped label keeps its icons beside its first line, and in a short window a menu opens upward only when it fits below the header, where the rail can show it, instead of tucking its top rows under the header.
